## Collaboration principles

SPIA is developed through human-led, multi-AI collaboration.

Contributions are recognized according to their actual participation. Suggestions, reviews, design concepts, and implemented code are different forms of contribution and should be documented accurately.

The project creator retains final decision-making authority.

**SPIA is a collaborative project, not the property of any individual AI model.**
